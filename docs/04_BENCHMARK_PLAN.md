# Benchmark Plan

## Benchmark Objective

Evaluate whether FSF can consistently stratify signal behavior under increasing levels of perturbation and biological heterogeneity.

## Synthetic Benchmarking

### Noise Experiments

Evaluate signal stratification under:

* Low noise
* Moderate noise
* High noise

### Subgroup Mixing

Evaluate behavior when multiple biological subgroups are pooled.

### Sign-Flip Scenarios

Evaluate signals exhibiting opposing responses across subgroups.

### Heterogeneous Population Structure

Evaluate stratification under unequal subgroup representation.

## Evaluation Metrics

Potential metrics include:

* Class assignment consistency
* Signal Stratification Index (SSI)
* Stratum stability frequency
* Perturbation robustness

## Real Data Validation

### UV Stress

Role:
Subgroup conflict model.

### Desiccation

Role:
Magnitude divergence model.

### Gamma Radiation

Role:
Temporal divergence model.

### Heat Stress

Role:
Relatively homogeneous control condition.

### Cold Stress

Role:
Relatively homogeneous control condition.

### Osmotic Stress

Role:
Relatively homogeneous control condition.

## Comparative Analyses

Compare:

* Signal strata composition
* Stability distributions
* Biological interpretation across conditions

## Success Criteria

FSF should demonstrate:

* Reproducible signal classification.
* Robust behavior under perturbation.
* Biological interpretability.
* Consistent stratification across repeated runs.
